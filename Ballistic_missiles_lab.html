📖 Ballistic Trajectory Physics & ISA Model

Earth Curvature & Circular Geometry

Earth's surface is modeled as a true circle ($R_E = 6,371\text{ km}$). Altitude rings are rendered as concentric circles centered at Earth's geometric center:

x_world(t) = (R_E + h(t)) * sin(S(t) / R_E)
y_world(t) = (R_E + h(t)) * cos(S(t) / R_E) - R_E

Fetter Aerodynamic Drag Approximation

Based on Steve Fetter's "A Ballistic Missile Primer" (1990), aerodynamic drag coefficient $C_d$ varies dynamically with Mach number ($M = v / a(h)$):

Subsonic (M < 0.8): C_d ≈ 0.15 - 0.18
Transonic (0.8 ≤ M ≤ 1.2): C_d peaks up to ~0.38 - 0.40 at M=1.1
Supersonic (1.2 < M ≤ 5.0): C_d decays as ~ 0.15 + 0.23 / √M
Hypersonic (M > 5.0): C_d asymptotes to ~0.15

Thrust & Mass Flow Rate

During the powered acceleration stage, mass flow rate is calculated directly as:

dm/dt = Thrust / v_e
